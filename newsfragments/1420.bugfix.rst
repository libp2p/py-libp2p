yamux: release the per-connection ``stream_backlog_semaphore`` slot when a stream is closed or reset. Slots were previously consumed permanently, so any connection that opened more than 256 streams over its lifetime — e.g. the bitswap client opening one wantlist stream per batch while transferring a large (multi-GB) file — would block forever in ``open_stream`` and hang the transfer. Bitswap response readers are now also scoped to the CIDs their own wantlist covered: they close their stream once that batch is complete instead of lingering until the whole transfer finishes, and no longer report other streams' pending blocks as missing.
