De-flake the ``dht_pair`` fixture in ``tests/core/kad_dht/test_kad_dht.py``. It now waits on a bounded poll (``trio.fail_after``) until each node is in the other's routing table **and** both signed peer records have reached the peerstores, instead of a fixed ``trio.sleep(0.1)`` followed by one-shot assertions. Under CI load the fixed wait raced: the routing-table entries (populated by the background refresh manager) or the peerstore envelopes (exchanged via the startup FIND_NODE) were not always ready, causing spurious ``AssertionError`` failures during fixture setup and in dependent tests.
