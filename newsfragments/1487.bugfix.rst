``Mplex.close()`` now closes the underlying secured connection even when the muxer is already shutting down. Previously it returned early once ``event_shutting_down`` was set, and the read loop's ``_cleanup()`` sets that flag on a peer-initiated EOF without closing the socket — so whenever the remote closed first, the local socket leaked (``ResourceWarning: unclosed socket``). This mirrors ``Yamux.close()``, which already closed unconditionally, and clears the residual stream-muxer socket leaks tracked in #1487.
